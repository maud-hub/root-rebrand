06 · Go to market

Hair leads.Body follows. Face next.

Sequence by demand signal, regulatory risk, and credibility transfer. Channels by trust before reach. Subscription mechanics from day one.

01 · Demand

It's the loudest pain.

~40% of women experience visible hair loss by age 40. Hormonally driven, undertreated, and culturally accelerating on TikTok.

02 · Risk

Cleanest regulatory lane.

Unambiguous cosmetic product. Stays out of OTC drug, Rx, and FDA-warning-letter territory the body cream brushes up against.

03 · Moat

Disclosed % out-credentials the field.

Divi's proof is n=31. Vegamour has no copper. Nutrafol has no copper. 2% GHK-Cu disclosed + 12-week phototrichogram trial is unmatched.

04 · Lever

Hair credibility transfers to body.

The customer who trusts ROOT on hair Q1 buys body Q3. Body-first would be a colder sell; hair-first warms the audience for everything after.
